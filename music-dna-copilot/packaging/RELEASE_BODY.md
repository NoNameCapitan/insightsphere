Music DNA Copilot, your private, cross-service Music DNA, as a desktop app. No Python to install.

| System | Download | Start |
|---|---|---|
| **Windows 10/11** | `music-dna-copilot-*-windows-x64.zip` | unzip → `Music DNA Copilot\Music DNA Copilot.exe` |
| **macOS (Apple Silicon)** | `music-dna-copilot-*-macos-arm64.zip` | unzip → move **Music DNA Copilot.app** to Applications |
| **Linux x64** | `music-dna-copilot-*-linux-x64.tar.gz` | extract → `music-dna-copilot/music-dna-copilot` |

**First start:** the builds are not code-signed.
- Windows: SmartScreen → **More info** → **Run anyway**.
- macOS: right-click the app → **Open** → **Open**.

**Your data** stays on your computer, in:
- Windows: `%APPDATA%\Music DNA Copilot`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/Music DNA Copilot`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/music-dna-copilot`

Updating = replacing the app; your data folder is kept.

**Connecting services:** Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Deezer, Last.fm and ListenBrainz connect read-only from the **Sources** page after a one-time setup. TIDAL, SoundCloud and others import export files.

Details: `DESKTOP_APP.md`, `CONNECTORS.md`, `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md` in the repository.
