# VetNear v1.17 — calm clinical premium

Release date: 2026-11-09

## Unified interface direction

- Applied the mineral-green, refined-teal clinical palette across the shared
  token system.
- Reduced decorative gradients, deep shadows, 3D movement and playful icon
  animation. Motion now mainly answers a user action and respects reduced-motion.
- Standardized cards around 20–24 px radii, subtle borders and near-flat depth.

## Home

- Rewrote the value proposition for immediate clarity.
- Kept one dominant action: “Знайти допомогу поруч”.
- Converted the urgent route into a restrained secondary action.
- Simplified the product preview: no wandering paws, floating service chips or
  beating-heart decoration; only a subtle pointer-responsive depth effect remains.

## Nearby and provider profiles

- Refined the list/map segmented control and the emergency information block.
- Reduced place cards to the two essential actions: call and route. Website and
  profile details remain available as quiet secondary links.
- Replaced category emoji on provider pages with precise service icons.
- Preserved the mobile sticky call/route bar and all alert availability rules.

## Partner journey

- Rebuilt provider onboarding as a three-step flow: essential information,
  services and contacts, final confirmation.
- Moved coordinates and secondary social links into progressive disclosure.
- Simplified the partner page around one CTA, three value statements, three
  understandable plans and a clear verification process.

## Safety retained

- No diagnostic or treatment claims were added.
- Emergency and 24/7 status still requires phone confirmation.
- Alert and shelter routing remain fail-closed when data is unavailable or stale.
